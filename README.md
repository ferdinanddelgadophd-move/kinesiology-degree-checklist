# Kinesiology Degree Checklist

An online degree checklist for students in the B.S. in Kinesiology at Wilberforce University. It follows the PT, OT, and Exercise and Sport Science tracks.

Students pick their track, check off courses as they finish them, and add their grade and term. The page shows what is left in General Education, the Kinesiology core, Kinesiology electives, and free electives, and students can print it or save it as a PDF for advising meetings.

## Privacy

The page does not collect or send any student information. Everything a student enters is saved only in their own browser on their own device. Students can download a backup file to move their checklist to another device.

## Official records

This checklist is a planning tool. The official degree audit comes from the Registrar's Office.
